    04 / BUILT FOR YOUR DESKTOP

    Make it your own.

    One key, your choice
    Open without holding the mouse. Send on key release, left click, or either—with no duplicate send.
    The center counts too
    Choose a center ping or emote and confirm without moving into an outer slice.
    Your GIFs. Your sound.
    Import GIF or animated WebP and pair a picture with your own MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A audio.
    Short clips with audio
    Use MP4 or WebM clips with embedded sound, up to 10 seconds.
    A wheel that fits you
    Change trigger keys, slices, center content, size, duration and volume in the redesigned settings studio.
    Across screens, with friends
    Retain multiple-display mapping and encrypted rooms for up to eight people. Custom media files stay local.

    Custom audio and video are limited to 10 seconds. Rooms do not distribute these files or custom sound; remote custom emotes keep the existing static placeholder behavior.
